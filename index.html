About Me

The editor behind the timeline

I edit YouTube videos with a focus on pacing, timing and making the footage feel natural.

I'm a freelance video editor mainly working with YouTube content. I like edits that move well, keep things clear and still feel like the creator behind them.

I spend a lot of time on pacing, sound design, small visual details and making sure each cut actually has a reason to be there.

The goal is pretty simple: make the video more enjoyable to watch without taking away what makes the creator's content their own.
